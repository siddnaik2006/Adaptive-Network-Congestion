Network Concepts Reference — Key components and mechanisms +
End Hosts (H1 – H4)

H1 transmits critical foreground traffic to H2. H3 transmits competing background traffic to H4 across the shared bottleneck link.

SDN Switches (S1 – S4)

OpenFlow-enabled switches that forward IP packets according to flow rules installed by the centralized controller.

Physical Links

Inter-switch connections characterized by transmission capacity (e.g. 10 Mbps) and propagation delay (e.g. 5 ms).

Buffer Congestion

Occurs when total traffic demand exceeds physical link capacity, filling switch queues and causing packet loss and increased latency.

AI Forecasting

A Ridge Autoregressive model examines 10-second history to forecast utilization 5 seconds ahead and compute empirical congestion risk.

Dynamic Dijkstra Routing

Computes the lowest-cost path with risk penalties, guarded by a 10s cooldown timer and 15% hysteresis to eliminate route flapping.
